An academy that complements your club

Special One Academy is a football technical training and coaching academy in Seville, run by Asociación Deportiva Andalucía Activa. We work with players from U7 to U19.

Our goal is not to replace clubs, but to complement the player's training with work that is closer, more detailed and more personal: small groups, specific sessions and training experiences designed to improve performance, game understanding and confidence on the pitch.

Our methodology is based on correction, purposeful repetition, adapted demand and learning through experience. And beyond training, we look for an environment where the player enjoys themselves, learns and develops alongside professionals committed to their progress.

SO Training Grupal

SO Training Grupal is the academy's permanent training program, with small-group sessions throughout the season.

  • Training throughout the season
  • Small groups by age, category and level
  • Individual feedback
  • A demanding, close environment

The aim is to complement club training, reinforcing technical, tactical, physical and mental aspects of the player.

SO Individual Training

Personalized sessions for players and goalkeepers.

What it is

SO Individual Training is the academy's individual training program, for players and goalkeepers who want a more personalized approach adapted to their needs.

What we work on

Unlike group work, each session focuses on specific goals: technical improvement, decision-making, coordination, body shape, striking, control, dribbling, finishing, defensive actions, confidence or position-specific skills. The coach gives direct feedback throughout the session.

Who it's for

For players who want to sharpen specific details of their game or get closer follow-up. Sessions are adapted to each player's age, level, position and goals, including goalkeepers.

Kit

Sessions are trained in the official SO Training kit, the same as in SO Training. The new kit will be required from 1 January 2027.

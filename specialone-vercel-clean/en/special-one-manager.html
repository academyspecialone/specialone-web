Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does the app cost?

Nothing. Special One Manager is free for the academy's families.

Can I keep booking through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can keep booking directly or through WhatsApp at the usual rates. The 2- and 4-session bundles of SO Training Grupal are cheaper when booked through Manager.

Is requesting a place the same as having it booked?

No. The booking is confirmed once the academy accepts it. You can see the status of each request in the app.

Do Manager rates apply to individual training?

No. They only apply to SO Training Grupal.

Do I have to download it from an app store?

No. It installs from your phone's browser, by adding it to the home screen.

How to install it on your phone once it's available

iPhone

Using Safari

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. If Open as Web App appears, turn it on. Tap Add.

Android

Using Google Chrome

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen or Install.
  3. Confirm the option shown to add the app to your phone.

Options vary by browser and operating system. Add the app, not this presentation page.

Special One Manager

Your academy, always at hand

Special One Academy's free app for families. Check training sessions, manage your players and request bookings from your phone, with no calls or messages.

And if you book your SO Training bundles through the app, each session costs less.

How to install it See how much you save Open the app

Install it on your phone in a minute

No need to download it from a store. It installs from your browser and stays on your home screen like any other app.

iPhone

  1. Open Special One Manager in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon is now on your phone.

Android

  1. Open Special One Manager in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, top right.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm. The icon is now on your phone.

The link to open the app will appear here on launch day.

Frequently asked questions

If you still have questions, message us on WhatsApp.

How much does the app cost?

Nothing. Special One Manager is free for the academy's families.

Can I keep booking through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can keep booking directly or through WhatsApp at the usual rates. The 2- and 4-session SO Training bundles are cheaper when booked through the app.

Is requesting a place the same as having it booked?

No. The booking is confirmed once the academy accepts it, and you can see the status of each request in the app.

Do the app's rates apply to individual training?

No. They only apply to SO Training.

Do I have to download it from an app store?

No. It installs from your phone's browser by adding it to the home screen. See the steps above.
